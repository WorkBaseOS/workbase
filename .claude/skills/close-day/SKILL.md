# Close the day

Two questions, in the owner's words, then one entry in `HANDOFF.md`. Nothing else changes.

1. Ask, if they haven't already said it: **"Where do things stand?"** — a few sentences, theirs.
2. Ask: **"What do you want to pick up next time?"** — a short list. Read the previous entry's "Next time" first and ask which of those are done; the rest carry forward.
3. Write today's entry at the top of `## Entries` in `HANDOFF.md`, in this shape and in their words, not yours:

   **<date>**
   Where things stand: …
   Next time: … (carried-forward items first, then new ones)

4. Say in one line that it's written. The project overviews are not touched unless the owner says "and record … in the project".
