# Close the day

Two questions, then one entry at the top of `## Entries` in `HANDOFF.md`, in the owner's words — never yours. Nothing else changes.

1. **"What's on your plate right now?"** Read the previous entry's list first and ask which of those are done; the rest carry forward.
2. **"What do you want to pick up next time?"**

Write:

**<date>**
On my plate: …
Next time: … (carried-forward items first)

Then one line: it's written. The project overviews are not touched unless the owner adds "and record … in the project".
