# Audit

Three checks, read from the folders, their documents, the tray, and `HANDOFF.md`. Change nothing until the owner picks.

1. **Finished but not archived.** A project whose documents or handoff say the work is done, or whose deadline is well past with nothing after it, still sitting under `03-projects/`. Everything there is ongoing by definition; finished work belongs in `99-archive/`.
2. **Sat too long — for the owner to look at, not a verdict.** Tray items older than a week, and a tray item that holds commitments nobody has recorded (a meeting recording, a call note) - the remedy is to process the tray, never to record them from here; a project with no change in its files or its handoff for several weeks; a commitment in a document whose date has passed with nothing recorded after it. A quiet project may be waiting appropriately, and a busy one may still hold an overdue promise — say what you see and let the owner judge.
3. **Contradicts itself.** Two documents, a document and a tray item, or either of them and a page in the brain's wiki (`00-brain/wiki/`), that disagree on one fact — a date, a number, a status. The wiki's own consistency is checked elsewhere; here the question is whether the work or the tray says something a page does not. When a tray item and a page disagree, the remedy is to process the tray and settle the fact with the owner, never an edit from the unprocessed item.

Report in plain sentences: the one thing to look at first, then each finding with what to do, then any question only the owner can answer. Five findings at most in the chat; the rest go in the saved report. Save the report as `99-archive/audits/<date>-audit.md` (create the folder if needed), then stop.

On the owner's word: archive what they name, keeping the folder's path — and nothing else. A contradiction is theirs to resolve; a stale commitment is theirs to close.

Evidence, not presence — say what you read. The owner decides. No score.
