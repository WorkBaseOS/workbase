# Start here

Welcome to **WorkbaseOS** — your AI business workspace in Claude or Codex. Set it up once. Put it to work every day.

## What this is

One place where your assistant knows your business, keeps the state of your work current, and helps you run your week — instead of starting from zero in every chat. Folders you can open and read; plain files that are yours, with no separate task-management app required.

Your Workbase is your *personal* workspace — and your second brain for the business: it remembers what was decided, promised, and changed better than you can. It works alongside the shared systems your team relies on — your drive, your CRM, your pipeline sheet: you prepare and decide here; what the team needs lives there.

## First steps

1. Open this folder in the **Claude Code app** or the **Codex app** (open the app, choose this folder). Both read the same rulebook: `AGENTS.md` holds the rules (Codex reads it natively), and `CLAUDE.md` points Claude to that same file — that's why both files sit at the root. We build and teach with Claude Code.
2. Say: **"Onboard me."** Your assistant interviews you briefly and fills `00-brain/` with your business — you answer questions and drag in documents; it does the typing.
3. From then on: toss anything into `01-in-tray/` as your day happens. When you're ready, go through it with your assistant, one item at a time — say what you want kept, it records it and files the item. Everything in operations and projects is ongoing; finished work moves to the archive — so the folders themselves are your list of open work. Once a week, say **"audit my Workbase"** for a tidy check. Pausing or switching work? Say **"save a handoff"** and answer one question; when you return, say **"where did I leave off?"** and you get a short recap. That's the habit; everything else grows from it.

## The rooms of your Workbase

**`00-brain/` — your Workbase's memory, and the foundation of everything else.** Everything your assistant should always know lives here: what your business is and offers, who your customers are, who is on your team, the words you use for things. Your assistant can read these facts when it needs them, so you don't have to explain your business from scratch each time. Getting this folder right helps everything else work better. Inside it you'll also find `house-rules.md` (explained below).

**`01-in-tray/` — the tray on your desk.** Anything that needs handling lands here: notes, screenshots, PDFs, exports, messages from your email or Slack that need follow-up. However it arrives — dragged in by you, saved from an app, or delivered by a connector you've set up — it gets handled the same way. Go through it with your assistant when you're ready — one item at a time, in your own words; nothing is recorded until you say so.

**`02-operations/` — the work that never ends.** Your ongoing responsibilities — marketing, finance, clients, the team — one folder per responsibility, holding the work itself.

**`03-projects/` — work with a defined finish.** A launch, a hire, a campaign — one folder each, holding the work itself — its plan, its notes, what came in — archived when done.

**`90-desks/` — your craft corner.** The specialist work you personally do — writing, analysis, design, code — gets a desk of its own, where the work (or the links to where it happens) stays connected to everything else.

**`99-archive/` — finished things.** Nothing is deleted here, ever. Done work moves in, stays findable, and stops cluttering your active workspace.

## What's ours and what's yours

Everything here is yours — every file can be edited, by you or by your assistant on your behalf. **Template updates never overwrite your customizations or files:** changed by you → never replaced; unchanged from what we shipped → updated freely; created by you → not ours to touch, ever.

**One tip about `00-brain/house-rules.md`:** that file is for anything you want your assistant to *always* do your way — "keep my client emails short," "always show me a draft before anything goes out," "never plan calls before 10." Just tell your assistant a rule like that once and it will record it there. Keep such rules in house-rules.md rather than editing `AGENTS.md` (the template's own rulebook): that way, improvements we make to the template's rules in future versions can still reach you.
