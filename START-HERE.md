# Start here

Welcome to your Workbase — your AI business workspace in Claude. Set it up once. Put it to work every day.

## What this is

One place where your assistant knows your business, keeps the state of your work current, and helps you run your week — instead of starting from zero in every chat. Folders you can open and read; plain files that are yours; no other tools required.

## First steps

1. Open this folder in the Claude Code app (open the app, choose this folder).
2. Say: **"Onboard me."** Your assistant interviews you briefly and fills `00-brain/` with your business — you answer questions and drag in documents; it does the typing.
3. From then on: toss anything into `01-in-tray/` as your day happens, and say **"process my tray"** when you're ready. Ask **"plan my week"** on Monday. That's the habit; everything else grows from it.

## The rooms of your Workbase

- `00-brain/` — what it knows: your business facts, your house rules, and `private/` for your eyes only.
- `01-in-tray/` — the tray: toss things in all day, process when ready.
- `02-operations/` — ongoing responsibilities, no finish line.
- `03-projects/` — work with an end date.
- `90-desks/` — your craft corners.
- `99-archive/` — finished things; nothing is ever deleted.

## What's ours and what's yours

Everything here is yours — every file can be edited, by you or by your assistant on your behalf. **Template updates never overwrite your customizations or files:** changed by you → never replaced; unchanged from what we shipped → updated freely; created by you → not ours to touch, ever.

One tip: put your standing preferences in `00-brain/house-rules.md` rather than editing `AGENTS.md`, so improvements to the template's rules stay eligible to reach you.
