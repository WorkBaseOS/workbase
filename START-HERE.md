# Start here

Welcome to **WorkbaseOS** — your AI business workspace in Claude or Codex. Set it up once. Put it to work every day.

## What this is

One place where your assistant knows your business, keeps the state of your work current, and helps you run your week — instead of starting from zero in every chat. Folders you can open and read; plain files that are yours, with no separate task-management app required.

Your Workbase is your *personal* workspace. It works alongside the shared systems your team relies on — your drive, your CRM, your pipeline sheet: you prepare and decide here; what the team needs lives there.

## First steps

1. Open this folder in the **Claude Code app** or the **Codex app** (open the app, choose this folder). Both read the same rulebook: `AGENTS.md` holds the rules (Codex reads it natively), and `CLAUDE.md` points Claude to that same file — that's why both files sit at the root. We build and teach with Claude Code.
2. Say: **"Onboard me."** Your assistant interviews you briefly and fills `00-brain/` with your business — you answer questions and drag in documents; it does the typing.
3. From then on: toss anything into `01-in-tray/` as your day happens, and say **"process my tray"** when you're ready. Ask **"plan my week"** on Monday. Finished something or changed direction? Tell your assistant so it can keep your overview current. That's the habit; everything else grows from it.

## The rooms of your Workbase

**`00-brain/` — your Workbase's memory, and the foundation of everything else.** Everything your assistant should always know lives here: what your business is and offers, who your customers are, who is on your team, the words you use for things. Your assistant can read these facts when it needs them, so you don't have to explain your business from scratch each time. Getting this folder right helps everything else work better. Inside it you'll also find `house-rules.md` (explained below) and `private/` (for sensitive information you wouldn't share with a team member).

**`01-in-tray/` — the tray on your desk.** Anything that needs handling lands here: notes, screenshots, PDFs, exports, messages from your email or Slack that need follow-up. However it arrives — dragged in by you, saved from an app, or delivered by a connector you've set up — it gets handled the same way. Say "process my tray" and your assistant organizes what you've added, updates the state of your work, and asks when something needs clarification.

**`02-operations/` — the work that never ends.** Your ongoing responsibilities — marketing, finance, clients, the team — one folder per responsibility, each with its own overview of where things stand.

**`03-projects/` — work with a defined finish.** A launch, a hire, a campaign — one folder each, with its own overview and a deadline where relevant, archived when done.

**`90-desks/` — your craft corner.** The specialist work you personally do — writing, analysis, design, code — gets a desk of its own, where the work (or the links to where it happens) stays connected to everything else.

**`99-archive/` — finished things.** Nothing is deleted here, ever. Done work moves in, stays findable, and stops cluttering your active workspace.

## What's ours and what's yours

Everything here is yours — every file can be edited, by you or by your assistant on your behalf. **Template updates never overwrite your customizations or files:** changed by you → never replaced; unchanged from what we shipped → updated freely; created by you → not ours to touch, ever.

**One tip about `00-brain/house-rules.md`:** that file is for anything you want your assistant to *always* do your way — "write my drafts in Dutch," "keep my emails short," "never plan calls before 10." Just tell your assistant a rule like that once and it will record it there. Keep such rules in house-rules.md rather than editing `AGENTS.md` (the template's own rulebook): that way, improvements we make to the template's rules in future versions can still reach you.
