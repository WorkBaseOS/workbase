# 02-operations — the work that never ends

Your ongoing responsibilities live here — the parts of your role with no finish line, whether you run a business, a department or a team: sales and marketing, finance and admin, clients and delivery, your team and its people, the output your unit is measured on, suppliers and purchasing, compliance and contracts, the tools and systems you run on, your stakeholders and your reporting upward. One folder per operation, named for the responsibility.

**What belongs here:** work you'll still be doing next quarter.
**What never belongs here:** a project — a defined piece of work with its own goal and its own finish. A deadline inside a responsibility does not make a project: the monthly update, the quarterly report, tomorrow's answer to your boss are dated items in the operation's own documents.
**The one rule:** the folder holds the work itself — your working documents and what came in. A commitment or a deadline lives in the document it belongs to. The folder is created when the first piece of work for it arrives, usually from the tray; ask your assistant and it makes one properly. Not sure what something is? A responsibility you will still have next quarter → operation. A defined piece of work with its own goal and an end → project. Your own craft → desk.
