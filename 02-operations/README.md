# 02-operations — the work that never ends

Your ongoing responsibilities live here — the parts of the business with no finish line: marketing, finance, client care, the team. One folder per operation.

**What belongs here:** work you'll still be doing next quarter.
**What never belongs here:** work with an end date — that's a project.
**The one rule:** the folder holds the work itself — your working documents and what came in. A commitment or a deadline lives in the document it belongs to. Say **"set up an operation for …"** and your assistant creates the folder. Not sure what something is? Never ends → operation. A finish line → project. Your own craft → desk.
