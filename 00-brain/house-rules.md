# House rules

Your standing preferences, in your own words. Your assistant always applies them.

What belongs here: anything you would otherwise repeat. The language of your drafts, when it differs from the language you chat in. What to always do or never do in your writing: voice.md shows how you write, a rule here says what to avoid or insist on. How things are formatted where it matters for the place you paste them. Where you want to be asked more than the rules already ask. Tools you prefer for a job. Tell your assistant a rule once ("keep my client emails short") and it records it here.
