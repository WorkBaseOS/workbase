# private — sensitive information

Anything you would not share with a team member lives here, and only here — notes on difficult conversations, sensitive plans, things you're not ready to share. You decide.

**The one rule:** ask yourself *"would I be comfortable if a team member read this?"* If the answer is no, it goes here. Your assistant may use it for your own work; before anything based on it is shared, it checks the intended audience with you.
