# 00-brain — what your Workbase knows

Stable facts live here: who you are, what your business is and offers, who your customers and team are, how you like to work. Your assistant reads this folder so you never have to explain your business twice.

**What belongs here:** facts that stay true for months — profiles, offerings, customers, team, terminology.
**What never belongs here:** tasks, project status, drafts, or anything in motion — those live in operations and projects.
**The one rule:** when a fact about the business changes, it changes here.

Two special files live in this folder:
- `house-rules.md` — your standing preferences, in your words. Your assistant always applies them.
- `private/` — for anything you would not show a team member. Ask yourself exactly that question; if the answer is no, it goes in there.
