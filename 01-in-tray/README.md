# 01-in-tray — the tray

Toss things here all day without breaking stride: meeting notes, a screenshot, a PDF, an exported report, half a thought in a text file, an email or Slack message that needs follow-up. Anything, any format, by any route — drag it in yourself, save it from an app, or have a connector you've set up deliver things here. Once something lands, it becomes part of your work like everything else.

**What belongs here:** whatever arrived and isn't handled yet.
**What stays here:** nothing — the tray is a buffer, not a home.

**How it's handled:** you and your assistant go through it together, one item at a time. Say what you want done with an item, in your own words; it records that in the working document it belongs to — a deadline in the plan, a decision in the notes — and moves the item into the project, operation or desk it belongs to — an existing one, or a new one you name — or into the archive when it is only to be kept, as with an audit report once read. Nothing is recorded unless you say so — it may suggest, in a sentence. It never guesses a date, an owner, or a completion; if something is unclear it asks, and the item stays in the tray. A day named in the item — "Monday", "end of the month" — is written with its date, counted from the item's own date, and a day you name while handling it is counted from today; that is reading, not guessing. An item identical to one already handled is named as such, moved to the archive, and recorded nowhere a second time.

**From the chat:** drag a file into the conversation, or type a note, and say "to my tray" — it saves it here for later. Say "deal with this" and it is handled on the spot.
