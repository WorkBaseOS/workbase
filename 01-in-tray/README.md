# 01-in-tray — the tray

Toss things here all day without breaking stride: meeting notes, a screenshot, a PDF, an exported report, half a thought in a text file. Anything, any format.

**What belongs here:** whatever arrived and isn't handled yet.
**What stays here:** nothing — the tray is a buffer, not a home. Say **"process my tray"** and your assistant files everything where it belongs and updates the state of your work.
**The one rule:** you never have to decide where something goes — that's what processing is for. (Filing things directly yourself is fine too; the tray is just the no-thinking-required option.)
