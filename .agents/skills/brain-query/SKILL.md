# Brain query

The wiki in `00-brain/wiki/` holds what has been ingested and what was filed back; read its `README.md` once if you have not this session.

## The steps

1. **Read `index.md` first** to find the pages that matter. Open them and follow their links as far as the question needs. When the question touches the business itself, read the profile files in `00-brain/` too.
2. **Answer in plain sentences**, citing the page each point comes from and, where it matters, the dated source behind the line. When two pages disagree, show both with their dates. Say plainly what the wiki does not hold.
3. **Know the wiki's edge.** It holds knowledge, and what each source said at the time, not the state of the work: what is due, what is open, what changed this week lives in operations, projects, and the tray, which this skill does not read. When a question is about work-state, say so and where the answer lives. A date on a source page is what that source said at the time, not the schedule as it stands; cite it as such.
4. **Offer to file a good answer back.** When the answer is a synthesis no page states - a comparison, an analysis, a connection between pages - offer to keep it: a page in `analysis/` in the shape the README gives, named for its subject, with the question and its date, the answer, and links to the pages it drew on - the same question asked again later adds a dated entry to that page rather than a second page; a line in `index.md`; `## [<date>] query | <question>` in `log.md`. On the owner's yes it is written, and each page it drew on gets one line at its end linking to it. A plain lookup - what one or two pages already say - is answered, not filed; what took several pages to put together and no page states is worth the offer.

## Rules that hold

- Nothing invented. If the pages do not say it, the answer says that.
- Lines marked as not for everyone serve the owner's own work; before anything derived from them is drafted for others, the audience is checked with the owner.
- Reading is not writing. Nothing in the wiki changes except an answer filed back on the owner's word.
