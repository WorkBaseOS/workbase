# Write my update

Turn the work as it stands into an update someone will read, in the owner's voice, ready to paste into their own tool. Nothing is sent from here and nothing is written.

## Settle the reader and the period — two questions at most

- **Reader:** who receives it, and which kind — upward (a manager, a board), outward (a client, a partner), across (the team). From the request when named; ask otherwise. `00-brain/team.md` says who people are; `00-brain/clients.md` who the client contacts are.
- **Period:** from the request ("last week" means Monday to Sunday; "this week" mid-week means Monday to today for what happened and today to Sunday for what's next); otherwise ask, one line.
- **Scope:** the project or operation the request names. Outward: everything about that client. Say the scope in a header line so the owner can narrow it.

## Read

The container's working documents (the plan, the notes, the outline — where decisions, deadlines, and commitments live), what came in during the period, and its section in `HANDOFF.md`. `00-brain/voice.md` for the register (external for outward, internal otherwise), `00-brain/house-rules.md` for language and length. If the tray holds items about this work, say so in the header: the draft may be behind them.

## Draft — five blocks, the owner's register

A header line for the owner: reader · period · scope. Then, as short paragraphs in the owner's register, no labels: what happened in the period; where it stands; what's next; what I need from you — whatever the reader must do, decide, or confirm; stuck or at risk — blockers wherever they sit, including our own side. Leave out a block with nothing in it. Never fold asks into progress or blockers into next steps.

**The voice samples give register only.** No phrase, date, or claim from a sample enters a draft.

## Check before handing over

Every sentence of substance traces to a document, a received item, the handoff, or the owner — never a guess. Anything about a person or a client's confidence stays out of an outward update and is named to the owner. No em dashes unless the owner's samples use them, no filler openers or closers, no bullets where the owner writes prose. The length the reader would actually read.

Hand it over with one sentence naming what you left out or are unsure of. If the owner adds a fact the documents don't hold, say so and offer to put it in the right document first.
