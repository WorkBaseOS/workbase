# Handoff — where I left off

Your own thread from one session to the next. At the end of a session, say **"closing for today"** (or /close-day): your assistant asks where things stand and what you want to work on next time, and writes your answers here as today's entry, newest on top, and carries forward anything from the last entry you said wasn't done. Next time, say **"where did I leave off?"** and it reads this back to you.

Nothing else changes when you close: the project overviews are only updated when you say "and record … in the project".
