# Handoff — where I left off

Saves where you stopped, so you can return later and know where to continue. Use it whenever you pause or switch work; it isn't tied to any schedule.

**Saving:** say "save a handoff" or "I'm stopping here". Your assistant asks one question — where are you leaving things, and what's the next step? — or, if the conversation already says it, drafts a summary and asks "Is this right?". It saves three things below, under the project or work you were on, and carries forward anything still open. It is where you paused, not the register of every promise: a deadline or a commitment stays in the project's own documents, even if you mention it here.

**Resuming:** say "where did I leave off?" and you get a brief recap: what you finished, what's still open, your next step. It only starts working if you ask.
